Quantum Error Correction • ML Routing

Latency-Constrained Meta-Decoding for QEC

Built a portfolio-based decoder router that evaluates complementarity between multiple decoders and selectively routes hard syndromes to rescue decoders under accuracy/latency trade-offs.

  • Portfolio of 6 decoders: heuristic, MWPM-like, Union-Find proxy, empirical MLD, MLP, CNN.
  • Best fixed baseline: CNN at 0.762687 accuracy; oracle portfolio upper bound: 0.995875.
  • Conservative rescue router achieved positive net gains over best fixed decoder with bootstrap validation.
