Quantum Error Correction • Meta-Decoding • ML Routing

Latency-Constrained Meta-Decoding for Quantum Error Correction

Built a portfolio-based decoder-routing benchmark to study when different decoders succeed on different syndrome regimes, and whether a learned/conservative router can exploit decoder complementarity without introducing harmful switches.

  • Implemented a six-decoder portfolio: heuristic, MWPM-like, Union-Find proxy, empirical MLD, MLP, and CNN decoders.
  • Designed router diagnostics around oracle accuracy, specialist behavior, switching risk, bootstrap confidence intervals, and random-switch controls.
  • Observed positive net rescue gains over the best fixed decoder on a harder structured benchmark, with conservative switching and statistical validation.

Quantum Tomography • Robust Inference • Noise Mismatch

Quantum State Estimation under Model Mismatch

Developed simulation and reconstruction tools for studying quantum-state estimation when the assumed noise model is wrong, drifting, or structurally mismatched with the true channel.

  • Compared MLE-style reconstruction and learned estimators under amplitude/phase damping versus assumed depolarizing noise.
  • Tracked fidelity, negativity, entanglement failure, and reconstruction gaps across controlled noise grids.
  • Framed the problem as robust inference under unreliable likelihood assumptions, rather than only standard tomography.

Reinforcement Learning • Adaptive Control • Decoder Selection

Adaptive RL Controllers for Noisy Decoder Selection

Explored ML/RL controllers for compute-aware decoder selection under non-stationary noise, with emphasis on the accuracy-compute frontier rather than unconditional use of the strongest decoder.

  • Implemented MLP selectors, budget-aware controllers, actor-critic variants, and history-aware routing strategies.
  • Evaluated controller behavior under drifting, bursty, and structured noise regimes.
  • Used repeated seeds and streaming-style experiments to inspect stability, collapse modes, and compute allocation.
